Privacy by Design

No servers. No uploads. No tracking.

🔒

Files never leave your device

All conversion and OCR happens inside your browser using WebAssembly and JavaScript. There is no upload endpoint.

🧠

OCR runs locally

Tesseract.js downloads language models once and processes images/PDFs entirely on your CPU/GPU. Nothing is sent to any server.

📡

No accounts • No analytics

We don’t collect emails, IPs, or usage data. The site is static and can even work offline after the first visit (as a PWA later).

🌐

Open Source

You can inspect every line of code on GitHub. Host it yourself, fork it, or audit it. Full transparency.

Technical reality: Libraries (PDF.js, Tesseract.js, etc.) are loaded from public CDNs on first visit. After that, your browser cache keeps them. Your actual documents are processed only in memory and never transmitted.
